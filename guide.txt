1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & BRAND ETHOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Veloce Marina Atelier Singapore (VMA) redefines car rental from an uninspired utility
counter into an ultra-premium, digital-first mobility atelier. Tailored for Singapore's
distinct automotive landscape (ERP 2.0 electronic tolls, Certificate of Entitlement [COE]
exclusivity, SP Mobility high-speed EV charging, and Johor/Malaysia cross-border
causeway corridors), the platform merges supercar sensations, executive electric
sedans, and luxury VIP MPVs.

Key Differentiators:
- Pure transparency in SGD pricing with explicit GST (9%), security deposits, and
  zero-excess insurance waivers.
- Full Singapore topology: Changi Airport Jewel 24/7 curbside valet, Marina Bay
  Sands VIP SkyDrive Pavilion, Orchard Paragon Luxury Vault, One-North Tech Bay,
  Sentosa Cove Marina Berth, and Woodlands Cross-Border Gateway.
- Integrated Cross-Border VEP (Vehicle Entry Permit) & Touch 'n Go tracking for
  seamless trips across the Causeway or Tuas Second Link into Malaysia.
- Real-time telemetry specs: 0-100 km/h acceleration, battery range, horsepower,
  luggage capacity, and minimum age restrictions.

3. JSON DATA STRUCTURE & RELATIONAL SCHEMAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The file `veloce_marina_singapore_dataset.json` contains:

1. platformMetadata:
   Global configuration: GST 9%, Singapore jurisdiction, ERP 2.0 system status.

2. fleet (Array of 8 High-Spec Vehicles):
   - id: 'CAR-SG-01' through 'CAR-SG-08'
   - make, model, year, category
   - powertrain, rangeKm, acceleration0To100, transmission, seats, luggageCapacity
   - dailyRateSGD, weekendRateSGD, securityDepositSGD, excessSGD, zeroExcessAddonDailySGD
   - malaysiaEntryAllowed, malaysiaEntrySurchargeDailySGD
   - erpOnboardUnit, includedMileageKmPerDay, extraKmRateSGD
   - chargingAccess (SP Mobility, Shell Recharge, Tesla Supercharger)
   - availableLocations: Array referencing `hubs.id`
   - telemetryFeatures: Suspension, audio, track systems
   - minimumAge, minimumDrivingExperienceYears

3. hubs (Array of 6 Singapore Pickup/Return Locations):
   - id: 'LOC-SIN-01' through 'LOC-WDL-06'
   - name, district, address, postalCode, mrt proximity, hubType, operatingHours, phone
   - amenities: Valet, shower suites, flight meet & greet, EV hyperchargers

4. addOns (Array of 5 Protection & Luxury Add-ons):
   - id: 'ADD-ZERO-EXCESS', 'ADD-MY-PASS', 'ADD-CHAUFFEUR', 'ADD-CHILD-SEAT', 'ADD-ROOFTOP-CARRIER'
   - name, dailyCostSGD, description, category

5. conciergeTeam (Array of 3 Staff Concierges):
   - id, fullName, role, baseHub, languages, phone, avatarUrl, bio

6. reservationSchedules (Over 2,600 relational calendar slots):
   - bookingId, carId, pickupLocationId, date, status ('CONFIRMED_RESERVED' vs 'AVAILABLE_FOR_DISPATCH')
   - allocatedPlate (Singapore format: e.g., SBA 1284Z)

7. patientReviews (Customer Experiences):
   - Real Singapore resident feedback on Marina Bay driving, cross-border Tuas Second Link trips, and Changi Jewel valets.

4. KEY USER JOURNEYS & INTERACTIVE FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Interactive Cockpit Fleet Browser:
   Filter by Category (Performance EV, Exotic Supercar, VIP MPV, Executive Sedan),
   Transmission, Location Proximity, and Cross-Border Malaysia capability.

2. Dynamic Live Singapore Cost & Toll Estimator:
   Computes: [Daily Rate * Days] + [Weekend Surcharge] + [Zero-Excess Add-on] +
   [Malaysia VEP Surcharge if selected] + [9% GST] = Total SGD with refundable deposit.

3. Cross-Border Causeway Route Planner:
   Dedicated workflow highlighting Woodlands Hub and Tuas options, automated VEP
   RFID tag confirmation, and pre-loaded Touch 'n Go balance.

4. 3-Step Express Dispatch Reservation:
   Select Dates & Location -> Add Extras (Zero Excess, Chauffeur) -> Driver License
   & Singpass/Passport Verification Screen -> Confirmed Dispatch Plate & Digital Key Pass.
